Questions

The fine print, in plain words.

What's not included?

Ad spend, SMS and AI usage fees, stock photography, copywriting beyond the pages in scope, and new features after delivery. Those go through a care plan or a new quote.

How do payments work?

50% to start, 30% at the agreed milestone, 20% on delivery. Card or ACH through Stripe. Care plans are billed monthly.

Is there a warranty?

Yes. Bugs in delivered scope are fixed free for 30 days after handover. Changes to scope are quoted.

Can my existing agency keep doing my marketing?

Yes, and that's common. I build the site and the system; your marketing partner sends traffic to it. I'm happy to work directly with them.
